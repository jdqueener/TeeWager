Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

TeeWager ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

TeeWager is designed to work without an account for free use. No account is required to start a round, track scores, or settle up. If you upgrade to TeeWager Pro on the web, an account is created to sync your subscription status across devices. On iOS and Android, Pro is purchased through the App Store and no account is required. We collect the following:

  • Round data — player names, scores, bean totals, and game results you enter during a round. This is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.
  • Course search queries — when you search for a golf course, your search query is sent to Golf Course API (golfcourseapi.com) to return course results and yardage data.
  • Purchase information — if you subscribe to TeeWager Pro, your purchase is processed by Apple (via the App Store) or Stripe (on the web). We do not store your payment details. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status.
  • Account information (optional) — if you choose to sign in, we store your email address via Supabase to sync your Pro status across devices.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide scorecard and bean-tracking features
  • To look up golf course data and yardages
  • To verify and restore Pro subscription status
  • To communicate with you if you contact us for support

Third-Party Services

TeeWager uses the following third-party services:

  • Golf Course API — provides course search and yardage data. Privacy policy
  • RevenueCat — manages in-app subscription status. Privacy policy
  • Supabase — optional account storage for Pro status sync. Privacy policy
  • Stripe — payment processing on the web. Privacy policy

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Data is only shared with the service providers listed above as necessary to operate the app.

Data Retention

Round data is stored locally on your device and can be deleted by uninstalling the app. If you create an account, you may request deletion of your account data by contacting us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

TeeWager is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.
