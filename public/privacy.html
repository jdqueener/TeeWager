Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

TeeWager ("we," "us," or "our") operates the TeeWager web application at teewager.io. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use TeeWager:

  • Account information: When you sign up with Google or email, we receive your name and email address.
  • Round data: Scores, player names, wager amounts, and bean totals you enter during a round. This data is stored locally on your device and, if you are signed in, synced to our servers so your round persists across sessions.
  • Usage data: Number of rounds completed, used to determine trial eligibility and Pro status.
  • Payment information: If you purchase a Pro plan, payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store your card number or payment details — Stripe handles all payment processing.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the TeeWager app
  • To determine trial eligibility and manage Pro subscriptions
  • To restore your in-progress round if you return to the app
  • To contact you about your account if necessary

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

TeeWager uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting
  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Google Sign-In — optional authentication method
  • Golf Course API — course and scorecard data

Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data.

Data Retention

We retain your account and round data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by emailing support@teewager.io.

Children's Privacy

TeeWager is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@teewager.io.
